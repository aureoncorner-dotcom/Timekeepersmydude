Demand ID: R09 - January selection against ECF 148.

WHY THIS ITEM EXISTS
ECF 148 (entered 29 December 2025) allows HHS/CMS to share plaintiff-state information with DHS/ICE only if it (1) is from the Medicaid program, (2) pertains only to aliens who are not lawfully residing in the United States, and (3) is limited to citizenship and immigration status, address, phone number, date of birth and Medicaid ID. It also bars DHS/ICE from using HHS/CMS data for immigration enforcement unless the data meet those conditions. ECF 148 states that the prior injunction, ECF 98, remained in place only until 5 January 2026.

Alberto Briseno's 9 April 2026 declaration (ECF 155-4) states that:
- ICE sent a Privacy Act (b)(7) letter to HHS/CMS on 6 January 2026 that referenced ECF 148 (paras. 4-5, 8-9);
- CMS matched against ICE's 10 December 2025 list of approximately 7.6 million open EARM cases, which included people with removal proceedings initiated, people under investigation and people with executable final orders (paras. 7-8);
- the request described its population as "individuals with unsatisfactory immigration status" (para. 9);
- after learning of the parties' disagreement about whether some individuals fell within the injunction, ICE "determined that the parameters of the open EARM cases could have been further limited" (para. 13).

These references identify the records sought. They do not presume that the file contained people outside the order's categories.
