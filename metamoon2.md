5.2 The 23.44-degree transformation
Earth’s axial obliquity is a tilt of the equatorial plane relative to the ecliptic. A tropical-to-sidereal ecliptic longitude conversion, by contrast, normally subtracts an epoch-dependent common zero-point offset A(t), called an ayanamsa in zodiacal applications. The numbers are not interchangeable. [A2, A3, S1]
λsid,j(t) = λtrop,j(t) − A(t) ⇒ λsid,i − λsid,j = λtrop,i − λtrop,j
That identity is exact if all bodies are transformed with the same offset at the same time in the same ecliptic plane. Therefore pairwise ecliptic longitude separations, angular gap RMS and the three heptagon chord-family scores are invariant under that zodiac-zero rotation. It is a valuable no-effect control: the geometry must not change just because the sign names do.

8.1 State reduction and prediction
A reduced clock or calendar label need not be a closed predictive state. In a deterministic model ẋ=F(x), a retained description π supports its own autonomous successor only when π(x)=π(y) implies the projected successors also agree for every eligible same-label pair. For stochastic dynamics the appropriate condition compares entire next-observation probability laws. Successful prediction on one trace does not prove this for the whole domain. [G2, G4]
The correct repair is to add an available witness sufficient for the particular target, not to assume that the original scalar was secretly a complete physical state. Source positions, calendar address, reference-frame flags and phase histories form a practical augmented record; their sufficiency for a new prediction must still be tested.
9. The clock as a bifurcation machine
The geometry-corrected clock documents distinguish mean-field coherence, pairwise phase locking, finite-triad behavior and physical escapement. A model may have a clock hand rotating steadily even while its relative phases are locked. Likewise, repeated phase-section crossings can occur during unlocked drift and are not automatically coherent ticks. [C1, C2]
Pair model (Adler): dφ/dt = Δω − K sin φ
For this declared pair model, if K<|Δω| the relative phase runs; if K>|Δω| stable and unstable relative equilibria exist. The transition at equality is a saddle-node on the circle (subject to the chosen positive-K convention). In a symmetric triad the two independent relative phases live on T², and its generic locking onset can involve a sink–saddle pair. Delays, inertia or forcing may change the bifurcation class.
Triad relative state: φ = (θ₂−θ₁, θ₃−θ₁) ∈ T²
The clock therefore needs a declared common phase hand Θ, the relative configuration φ, the rates, and an event rule. Ticks are oriented crossings of the hand; slips require a specified relative-phase boundary; returns are witness-dependent; calendar resets arise from the date compiler. The source v0.4 also preserves a three-position physical-field dial under its own gauge-invariant model, but that model is not the planetary ephemeris.
If we merely convert tropical to sidereal longitude at one time, the physical source state has not changed and no new bifurcation has occurred.
