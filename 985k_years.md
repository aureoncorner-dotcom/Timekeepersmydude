The 985.5-kya Convergence Test

A geometric readout of what overlaps, what does not, and what would change the result

Status: Open, but not supported at the claimed temporal resolution.

Geometric summary: A target-induced star, two near-target temporal lobes, and no shared target point.

The current evidence does not converge on one historical event at 985.5 thousand years ago (kya). It produces a weaker but still useful structure: several dated ranges touch an investigator-chosen window around the target, while most of those ranges do not meet at the target and often do not overlap one another directly. Only one published range—the 2022 chromosome-2 estimate of 0.4–1.5 million years ago (Ma)—contains 985.5 kya. Because that interval is broad, assumption-sensitive, and opposed by a newer 5–7 Ma model, it establishes compatibility under one model, not confirmation of the target and not evidence of intervention.

That result does not close the hypothesis. It tells us what the current evidence can and cannot carry.

1. Keep three layers separate

Hypothesis. A distinctive event near 985.5 kya generated linked chromosome-2, demographic, hominin-population, archaeological, and Atrahasis-pattern signals.

Range overlap. A published date or uncertainty interval intersects the target point or one of the test windows. This is arithmetic compatibility. It does not identify a common cause, an agent, or even the same event.

Finding. The observation or inference that the cited source actually licenses. A finding retains its witness type, uncertainty, provenance, and methodological limits.

The table is useful precisely because these layers are not allowed to collapse into one another. The hypothesis selects what is being tested. Range arithmetic says where a source could fit. The finding says what was observed. A story may connect them only after an independent bridge is supplied.

2. Coordinate and test

The target coordinate is:

T0 = 985.5 kya, generated by the arithmetic 13,500 × 73 = 985,500 years.

That calculation generates a hypothesis coordinate; it is not itself a genetic, fossil, archaeological, or textual observation.

The test uses nested windows:

Tight core: 885.5–1085.5 kya (±100 kyr)
Context: 735.5–1235.5 kya (±250 kyr)
Broad context: 400–1500 kya, matching the full interval reported by the 2022 chromosome-2 timing model

A row becomes meaningful target support only if it supplies a source-backed finding with enough temporal specificity to discriminate the target from ordinary deep-time background. A stronger convergence would require at least two independent empirical lanes to identify a common uncertainty region near T0 without depending on the same specimen, site, model family, or myth-derived selection rule. Exact point equality is not required; direct cross-lane intersection, independence, and a defensible causal bridge are.

3. Lane-by-lane readout

Chromosome 2

Finding. Human chromosome 2 contains the expected relics of an ancestral telomere-to-telomere fusion. The fusion is a real biological event (Ijdo et al., 1991). Later work improves the structural resolution of the complex fusion region but does not supply evidence of design or agency (Poszewiecka et al., 2023).

Range overlap. A 2022 computational model estimates the fusion at roughly 0.9 Ma with a reported 95% interval of 0.4–1.5 Ma. T0 lies inside that interval (Poszewiecka et al., 2022).

Counterfinding. A newer analysis of segmental duplications and incomplete lineage sorting places the relevant fusion-site history early in African great-ape speciation, roughly 5–7 Ma (Yang et al., 2026). Archaic centromere organization also provides a phylogenetic lower-bound constraint rather than a 985.5-kya point date (Miga, 2017).

Effect on the hypothesis. The fusion is established. A recent date is model-dependent. The 2022 interval makes H5 range-compatible under one model; it does not confirm a 985.5-kya event. H4—intentional chromosome intervention—remains unsupported because none of these observations discriminates agency from natural chromosomal processes.

Demography

Finding. The FitCoal analysis infers a severe ancestral bottleneck from about 930 to 813 ka (Hu et al., 2023).

Range overlap. That interval overlaps the tight core but excludes T0. Its oldest bound, 930 ka, is 55.5 kyr younger than the target.

Counterfinding. Two independent critiques argue that the severe bottleneck is not stable across alternative demographic models or population subsets and may arise from non-identifiability or model misspecification (Cousins and Durvasula, 2025; Deng, Nielsen, and Song, 2025). A later defense reports simulations and compatible fossil context, but it comes from the same model family and is not an independent replication (Zhou et al., 2026).

Effect on the hypothesis. The demographic signal is near-date context and a live methodological dispute. It does not currently supply an independent anchor at 985.5 kya.

Hominin populations

Finding. The fossil record documents multiple hominin populations around the broader period. Daka Homo erectus is approximately 1 Ma (Asfaw et al., 2002). Gran Dolina TD6.2 is dated to roughly 772–949 ka, and proteomics places Homo antecessor as a sister lineage to later Middle and Late Pleistocene hominins (Duval et al., 2018; Welker et al., 2020). Thomas Quarry I hominins are much younger, near 773 ka (Hublin et al., 2026).

Range overlap. The Daka point and the TD6.2 interval touch the tight window, but neither identifies a unique event at T0. Daka is an approximate point; TD6.2 ends at 949 ka.

Redating control. Yunxian 2 had been analyzed under a roughly 1-Ma chronology (Feng et al., 2025). Direct burial dating now places the crania at 1.77 ± 0.08 Ma, removing them as a near-target timing hit while leaving the separate morphological questions open (Tu et al., 2026).

Effect on the hypothesis. The lane supports occupancy and lineage diversity across the Early–Middle Pleistocene. It does not identify a cousin dynasty, a replacement labor population, or a synchronized 985.5-kya transition.

Archaeology

Finding. Wonderwerk Cave preserves secure local burning evidence in a stratum dated approximately 1 Ma (Berna et al., 2012). A deeper Wonderwerk fire signal lies in a much wider 1.07–1.79 Ma interval (Marin-Monfort et al., 2026). Strong evidence of controlled fire at Gesher Benot Ya'aqov is younger, around 790 ka (Goren-Inbar et al., 2004). Acheulean technology is documented far earlier, by about 1.76 Ma (Lepre et al., 2011).

Range overlap. Approximate Wonderwerk dating places one local observation near T0. The other fire and tool-industry observations spread across the broad context and beyond it.

Effect on the hypothesis. The lane shows local, uneven behavioral evidence rather than a global or multi-regional transition centered on 985.5 kya.

Textual structure

Finding. Atrahasis contains the labor-burden, revolt, replacement-population, expansion, suppression, catastrophe, remnant, and revised-settlement sequence identified in the source hypothesis document.

Range overlap. None. The text supplies no Pleistocene date.

Effect on the hypothesis. The narrative structure is a legitimate object of analysis, but it is the source of the proposed pattern, not an independent empirical lane for dating that pattern. Its identification with a prehistoric event remains open until an independent provenance or prediction bridge exists.

4. The geometry

For a dated evidence row e with interval Iₑ, the spreadsheet first asks whether:

Iₑ ∩ W ≠ ∅

where W is one of the nested windows. That is a window-membership test. It is not the same as asking whether two evidence intervals intersect each other, whether several independent intervals have a common intersection, or whether they describe the same cause.

The current graph has three important features.

A broad apex

The 2022 chromosome-2 interval, 400–1500 kya, is the only dated empirical range that contains T0. It also intersects many otherwise separated rows. Geometrically it acts as a broad apex or universal bridge. Its width is what gives it connectivity, so that connectivity cannot also be counted as high temporal specificity.

Delete that apex and no remaining dated range contains T0.

Two near-target lobes and a gap

Without treating the broad chromosome interval as a precise center, the near-target rows divide into two visible lobes:

A younger lobe extending roughly 769–949 ka, containing Thomas Quarry I, TD6.2, the disputed 813–930 ka bottleneck, and the 790-ka fire control.
An approximate 1-Ma lobe containing Daka and Wonderwerk, plus the superseded legacy Yunxian point if it is improperly retained.

T0 = 985.5 kya lies in the 51-kyr gap between the TD6.2 upper bound at 949 ka and the approximate 1-Ma points. Those points touch the same ±100-kyr window, but they do not touch each other through the target.

No shared target simplex

No set of independent empirical lanes has a common intersection containing T0. There are off-target overlaps—for example, chromosome 2, demography, and TD6.2 can share part of the 813–930 ka region because the chromosome interval is so broad; chromosome 2, Daka, and Wonderwerk can meet at an approximate 1-Ma point. Neither is a multi-lane intersection at 985.5 kya.

The resulting topology is therefore:

target-induced star + two temporal lobes + no shared target point

5. Why the projection matters

Collapsing every row to “hits the tight window” creates an equivalence class. Rows with different dates, widths, methods, specimens, and claims receive the same downstream label. That is quotient-level equality: equality after information has been discarded.

It does not imply source-level equality or event identity upstream.

In the full space—time × evidence lane × witness type × provenance × claim role—the apparent convergence currently exists mainly in the time-window projection. The chromosome interval has high connectivity but low temporal resolution. The fossil and archaeological points have local specificity but weak causal connection. The textual pattern has structural relevance but no independent date. Keeping those coordinates visible prevents a broad interval or a compelling narrative from exporting support into claims it did not measure.

6. What this conclusion does—and does not—close

The conclusion is not “impossible,” “debunked,” or “stop looking.” It is:

Open, but not supported at the claimed resolution by the current evidence set.

That preserves room for revision while refusing to promote a window hit into a historical finding. Chromosome-2 fusion remains real. The 2022 timing overlap remains real. The FitCoal result and its dispute remain real. The fossils, fire evidence, and Atrahasis structure remain real. What is not yet real as a finding is the proposed linkage of those observations into one event at 985.5 kya.

Evidence that would materially change the result includes:

independent chromosome-2 timing methods that yield substantially narrower overlapping uncertainty around T0;
independent demographic methods recovering the same contraction across compatible population subsets;
a pre-specified, well-dated multi-regional fossil or archaeological transition centered near the target;
a source-independent text-to-prehistory bridge that makes risky predictions before evidence selection; or
a mechanism-specific signature that distinguishes deliberate biological action from natural chromosomal processes.

7. Next discriminating test

The next move is an apex-deletion nerve test with sliding-window controls:

Treat each dated evidence row as a vertex.
Draw an edge only when two intervals overlap directly and the observations are independent enough to count separately.
Count a higher-order simplex only when all member intervals share a common intersection; mark whether that intersection contains T0.
Recompute after deleting the broad 2022 chromosome-2 range, superseded chronology, same-specimen duplicates, same-site dependencies, and same-model-family responses in turn.
Slide T0 and the same nested windows across the 400–1500 kya context to test whether the observed connectivity is unusually concentrated at 985.5 or is an ordinary consequence of wide intervals and dense background evidence.

This test does not freeze the hypothesis. It asks whether the apparent center survives removal of its broadest bridge and whether 985.5 is geometrically exceptional relative to nearby coordinates.

8. Apex-deletion counterfactual — additive robustness record

Status: Completed. This counterfactual is additive. It does not alter or replace the baseline result in sections 1–7 or any baseline tab in the evidence ledger.

Scope and exclusions

Deleted body: C2-02, the UBCS-based 400–1500 kya chromosome-2 interval. No other row was deleted in this run.

HOM-04 remains visible in the source ledger but is excluded from the eligible graph because its approximate-1-Ma chronology is superseded by HOM-05. Undated rows remain evidential controls rather than dated vertices. The declared scan body is 400–1500 kya.

Baseline snapshot

Before deletion, the in-band graph had 9 eligible vertices, 13 direct-overlap edges, 11 cross-lane edges, one connected component of size 9, and 5 three-vertex common-intersection simplices. C2-02 had degree 8 and was the only interval containing T0. Even in that baseline, no shared edge or higher-order simplex contained T0.

Counterfactual graph

After deleting C2-02, the in-band graph has 8 vertices, 5 direct-overlap edges, 3 cross-lane edges, three connected components of sizes 4, 2, and 2, and no three-vertex common-intersection simplex. No surviving vertex, edge, or higher-order simplex contains T0.

Surviving direct intersections:

DEM-01 × HOM-02: 813–930 kya (cross-lane).
HOM-01 × ARC-01: 1000 kya (cross-lane).
HOM-02 × HOM-03: 772–777 kya (same lane).
HOM-02 × ARC-03: 790 kya (cross-lane).
ARC-02 × ARC-05: 1300 kya (same lane).

Deletion effect

Direct-overlap edges: 13 → 5, a 61.5% reduction.
Cross-lane edges: 11 → 3, a 72.7% reduction.
Largest connected component: 9 → 4.
Three-vertex common-intersection simplices: 5 → 0.
Target-containing intervals: 1 → 0; shared target simplices remain 0 → 0.

Sliding-window control

The coordinate and the ±100-kyr and ±250-kyr windows were slid from 400 to 1500 kya in 0.5-kyr steps, using the same current eligible dated evidence set after apex deletion.

At T0 = 985.5 kya, no interval contains the point and no cross-lane intersection passes through it. The ±100-kyr window touches 5 intervals across 3 lanes, but its induced graph has only 2 edges, a largest component of size 2, and no triangle. The raw five-hit maximum persists across 970–1030 kya, so it is a 60-kyr plateau rather than a unique center. Cross-lane point-sharing occurs at 790 kya, throughout 813–930 kya, and at 1000 kya—not at 985.5 kya.

The ±250-kyr window at T0 touches 7 intervals across 3 lanes, with 4 edges, a largest component of size 4, and no triangle. This is neighborhood density, not a shared target event.

Counterfactual verdict

Target-specific convergence does not survive apex deletion. What survives is fragmented, off-target context: a younger 769–949-kya lobe, an approximate-1-Ma cross-lane pair, and a same-lane 1300-kya pair. The original baseline remains unchanged: open, but not supported at the claimed temporal resolution. This robustness result does not make the hypothesis impossible and does not erase any licensed source finding.
